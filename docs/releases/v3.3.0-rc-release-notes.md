## For authors: what is new

### Your manuscript is safer
- **Search & replace can no longer damage a chapter.** Search, highlighting, the Replace All preview and the replacement itself use one text model, so formatting and special characters (`&`) are never corrupted, Replace One changes the occurrence you selected, and your latest typing is saved before any search or replace. A replace that would alter the chapter's formatting is refused and nothing is saved.
- **Manuscript import** (Write binder → "Import manuscript…", `.txt` or `.docx`, up to 25 MB): lines such as "Chapter 1" start new chapters; every chapter is saved before the dialog reports success, numbered after your existing chapters, which are never changed.
- **Dictated audio notes keep every word.** If the AI clean-up of a transcript would lose more than a tenth of your words, the original transcript is kept instead.
- **Sign-in is more secure.** Your session is an HttpOnly cookie that page scripts cannot read; signing out ends that device's session; changing your password signs out every other device. *Everyone signs in once more after upgrading.*
- **Account page:** change your password, or delete your account and everything in it (password plus typing DELETE; immediate and irreversible). The data policy is published at `/data-policy`.
- **Undo can no longer empty a chapter.** Opening a chapter is no longer an undo step, so pressing Ctrl/⌘Z repeatedly stops at the text as you opened it; before this fix it could roll back to an empty page (or the previously open chapter) and autosave would save that. Found and fixed in the 2026-10-06 review.
- **Backups** run every hour and are checked by a real test restore every day. *These backups stay on the server — see Known limitations §1.*

### Writing tools you control
- **Sentence locking:** lock sentences and rewrite around them; locked text is kept exactly.
- **Strength:** Light / Moderate / Strong for tone, audience and style rewrites (Light is the default). A Light rewrite that changes too much is retried once, and if it is still heavy you see a "changed more than expected" note. *Light for tone and audience is improved but awaits re-validation — see Known limitations §2.*
- **"No change needed":** text that already fits the request comes back unchanged with a reason.
- **Character names are protected:** a rewrite that loses a name is repaired once, otherwise you are warned which names are unconfirmed. A rewrite that adds a name not found in your text gets a gentle note (it never blocks).
- **Phase 3 AI workflow:** pin a result (free plan: kept 7 days), compare and merge versions, generate from a pinned version, "What the AI must keep" rules, the Idea Shelf (World → Notes → Ideas) with "N ideas waiting" markers in the binder, and similarity notes.
- **Author-inspired style rewrite** (public-domain authors only) and **copyright-risk check** shipped in v3.2.0. *The copyright disclaimer wording is still awaiting legal review.*

### A new workspace (Studio)
- Seven workspaces on the left rail — Write, Plan, Characters, World, Analyze, Assistant, Publish — with every tool in exactly one place. The right side is only the AI sidebar (Ctrl+\\ widens it).
- Resizable panels, your layout remembered per browser; Reading, Focus, Zen and Typewriter views; Draft and Edit modes; the command palette (Ctrl/⌘K). Works from 1920 down to 768 pixels wide.
- *The writing-first layout passed the owner-authorised agent review (2026-10-06); Compare versions and Search & replace are hard to discover (HR-14). Accessibility: the manuscript editor now has a name and role, a restored AI panel no longer takes focus on load, and the Story Bible content scrolls by keyboard; automated checks pass on every workspace, but a real screen-reader pass has not been done.*

### Answers grounded in your book
- **Plot Assistant** searches up to the chapter you are in by default, so it does not spoil later chapters; "Full manuscript" is one click. It says when something was not found *in the searched range* rather than "not in your story", and it shows which chapters it used.
- **Voice questions** follow the same chapter rule; the whole manuscript is searched only when you ask for it.
- **Story Bible** marks itself complete only when all five sections generated, cites a chapter for every entry, says "not established in the manuscript" instead of inventing, warns when your chapters have changed since it was generated, and no longer copies its own prompt examples into your bible.
- **Continuity and the Manuscript Report:** every continuity finding cites real chapters; a flashback marked in the text is no longer reported as a timeline error; the report is saved and shows stakes, plot weight, themes and open threads. Long chapters are now summarised in full (they were cut at about 1,400 words).
- **Characters:** possible duplicate characters are listed for you to merge or dismiss (nothing merges automatically); each character can be marked *On page*, *Mentioned only* or *In the past*; an optional "By importance" order.
- **Writing suggestions** read like developmental notes (weakness first, observation and recommendation, priority), without empty or duplicate items. Restored to the studio on 2026-10-06 (owner decision OD-09): Write → AI assistant → Generate → Suggestions. With no selection it reads the start of the chapter in whole sentences, and a claim that a quoted phrase is "repeated" is checked against your text. *The agent review found the advice still misses some weaknesses and invents problems in strong prose (HR-11).*
- **Writing analytics** explain every number and show genre benchmarks for 8 genres.
- **OCR** reads printed pages again (it had failed since the transformers upgrade); you review the text before it is added to a chapter. Handwriting quality is not measured.

### Fixed since v3.2.0 (selected)
Chapter continuation and outline (they failed outright); Story Intelligence (it had never run, and now reports failures honestly); plot-hole and continuity results that came back empty instead of partial; the voice agent reporting success for actions that did not happen; search results replaced by an older query; the selection toolbar covering Save; notes failing to load; a Plot Assistant spoiler leak through story memory; seven routes through which one author could reach another author's data (fixed and tested in Stage 9). Story questions (Plot Assistant and voice) about a chapter whose text tells an AI what to do now answer the question instead of following that text (found and fixed 2026-10-05).

## For operators

- **Deploy:** `bash start-narratiq.sh` on a RunPod pod with one ≥ 24 GB NVIDIA GPU (verified: 1× A40) and ports 3000 and 8000 exposed **at pod creation** (port contract: `docs/operations/runpod-deployment.md`). From scratch: about 15 minutes measured (2026-10-03).
- **Data:** PostgreSQL 16 + pgvector; schema head `0027` (the startup script applies pending migrations only after a pre-migration backup). Rolling back past `0027` loses the *On page / Mentioned only / In the past* labels (whether that counts as "without data loss" is an open owner decision).
- **Runbooks:** backup and restore (`backup-and-restore.md`, including the empty-database guard, §4a), rollback (`rollback.md`: code, schema, frontend, model, prompt version), incident response, monitoring and alerting, capacity planning, storage and persistence, environment variables — all under `docs/operations/`.
- **Behaviour changes:** exactly one backend worker (the backend refuses more); `/api/health` answers 503 while the model is unavailable; vLLM listens on 127.0.0.1 only; unprotected streaming rewrite routes are off (`AI_STREAM_ROUTES_ENABLED=false`); prompt set `v4` is the default (`PROMPT_VERSION=v3` restores the previous prompts with one restart); story Q&A marks the author's material as data in the prompt (`PROMPT_INJECTION_DATAMARK_QA=false` restores the previous Q&A prompt); `NARRATIQ_PERIODIC_BACKUP_RETENTION_COUNT` was replaced by `NARRATIQ_BACKUP_KEEP_RECENT` / `NARRATIQ_BACKUP_KEEP_DAILY_DAYS`.
- **Tests:** the one-command regression runner `backend/tests/run_full_regression.sh` (backend, retrieval, migration round trip, frontend unit, studio, accessibility, variants, live browser, AI invariants, documentation checks). There is **no CI**; record a full run for every release (waiver W-1, pending).

## Known limitations

The single list for this release candidate. Each item is in exactly one category. A limitation is "accepted" only
with a dated owner decision; an accepted limitation keeps its measured result, including criteria that were **not
met**. Decisions: `docs/testing/stage-12/stage-12.1/owner-decisions.md`.

### 1. Release-blocking operational condition

- **⚠ W-3 — no real author data until an off-pod backup is verified (owner, 2026-10-05: not waived).** Backups are
  written hourly to `/workspace/backups` on the RunPod network volume and verified by a real restore, but **no copy
  leaves the pod**. Loss of the RunPod network volume loses every on-volume backup, and an event that loses the pod
  and its volume together — this project's pod has been replaced three times (2026-09-21, 2026-10-03, 2026-10-05) —
  **can lose both the production database and all on-volume backups**; authors' manuscripts would be unrecoverable.
  **Hard condition: no real author manuscripts or data may be accepted or stored in production until an approved
  off-pod destination exists and at least one off-pod restore has been verified.** The hook
  (`NARRATIQ_OFFPOD_COMMAND`) exists; a provider needs the owner's separate approval. The daily check reports this
  every day until it is met.
